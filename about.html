About Jingwei

I am Jingwei Li, Ph.D., an AI and data science researcher with interdisciplinary training in computer science, applied mathematics, numerical analysis, statistics, and big data analytics. I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Colorado Boulder in May 2023 under the supervision of Professor Xiao-Chuan Cai. My dissertation was titled Domain Decomposed Learning Algorithms with Applications.

My research focuses on building scalable computational and statistical learning methods and turning them into real-world, data-driven solutions. My work includes domain-decomposed classification based on linear discriminant analysis, principal component analysis, AI-powered object and face recognition, and machine learning for intelligent image processing. I have also developed time-varying effect and mediation models using spline-based estimation to extract insight from complex longitudinal data.

Research themes

  • Mathematical foundations and algorithms for trustworthy, scalable machine learning.
  • Domain decomposition and parallel, big-data-ready computational methods.
  • Predictive and causal statistical modeling, including time-varying effects and mediation.
  • Deep learning, computer vision, and AI for biomedical image analysis.
  • High-performance and cloud computing for data-intensive scientific software.
