Semidefinite Programming

In this project, I proposed and implemented a warm-starting approach for interior-point methods for semidefinite programming, with the goal of accelerating large-scale semidefinite programming optimization, a core building block of modern data-driven and machine learning pipelines.

Related presentation: Li, J. & Engau, A. (2011). “Warmstarting Interior-Point Methods for semidefinite programming.” SIAM Conference on Optimization, Darmstadt, Germany.
