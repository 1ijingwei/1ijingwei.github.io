Domain-Decomposed Learning

This research line develops scalable, parallel-ready AI learning methods that combine domain decomposition with statistical classification and object recognition.

Dissertation

Domain Decomposed Learning Algorithms with Applications, Ph.D. dissertation, University of Colorado Boulder, 2023. Advisor: Prof. Xiao-Chuan Cai.

Selected publication

Li, J. & Cai, X.-C. (2024). “Domain decomposed classification algorithms based on linear discriminant analysis: An optimality theory and applications.” Neurocomputing, 575, 127261. DOI.

Earlier work includes a domain decomposition approach for object recognition, presented at the 25th Domain Decomposition Methods Conference (2018), and a project on domain decomposition for human face recognition presented at the 15th Copper Mountain Conference on Iterative Methods.
