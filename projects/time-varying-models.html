Time-Varying Statistical Models

This research focuses on data-driven estimation and inference for time-varying effects, including mediation effects, using flexible spline-based approaches for longitudinal and big health data.

Manuscript in preparation

Li, J., Piper, M.E. & Coffman, D. (2025). “An estimation approach for time-varying effect models using cubic splines.” Journal of Behavioral Data Science (in preparation).

Conference presentations

  • “Time-varying mediation effect models.” 6th National Big Data Health Science Conference, 2025.
  • “An estimation approach for time-varying effect models using cubic splines.” ISDSA 2024 Meeting, Vienna.
  • “An estimation approach for time-varying effect models.” Modern Modeling Methods 2024, Storrs, Connecticut.
