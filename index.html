Jingwei Li, Ph.D.

AI for Science · Agentic AI · Domain Decomposition · AI + HPC Convergence · Multimodal Biomedical AI

I am an AI and data science researcher building AI-native, scalable, and trustworthy machine learning for biomedical discovery. My core research brings domain decomposition, a principle from high-performance scientific computing, into statistical learning: domain-decomposed learning algorithms split large-scale problems into subproblems, solve them in parallel, and coordinate the results, so that learning scales across distributed and GPU-accelerated resources.

This sits within a broader program in scientific machine learning, including linear discriminant analysis, time-varying effect models, and computer vision, where transfer and contrastive learning help build robust representations for medical image analysis. I am now exploring how multimodal foundation models, agentic workflows, and AI co-scientist systems can plug into scalable, verifiable pipelines, moving biomedical research from data to discovery with greater speed, interpretability, and reproducibility.
